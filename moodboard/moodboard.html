One obvious step to get in

What this means for our design

Four decisions to carry into the first screens

Direct entry

The email link opens the invited proposal after a short code check. No account setup or generic dashboard detour.

Visible next step

Put review, reply, or sign above the file list. Keep completed and supporting items easy to find below.

Contextual conversation

Connect every passage comment to the right page and version; give phone readers a focused thread view.

Neutral, agency-owned

Reuse Outmarket components with neutral tokens. Show the agency identity throughout the portal and notifications.
